About Rusbi Group

A Heritage of Value, Innovation & National Impact

Founded by Alhaji Rufa'i Sule Bichi with a deep commitment to industrial advancement and grassroots empowerment, Rusbi Group has evolved into a multi-sector conglomerate. Operating out of Kano State, Nigeria, our operations span state-of-the-art fertilizer bulk blending, essential healthcare delivery, technical vocational education, and consumer goods.

Incorporated on August 17, 2018 (RC: 1519337) initially as Rusbi Agro-Chemicals & Allied Ltd, the group today under the executive leadership of Muhammad Rufai Sule serves farmers, commercial dealers, students, and industrial clients across Northern Nigeria and the Sahel corridor.

Our guiding philosophy remains simple: Nagari Na Kowa — true quality must serve everyone.

Sustainability & Impact

Empowering Communities, Sustaining Livelihoods

Our corporate social responsibility is built into our business model — not bolted on. Every division invests in the people who make agriculture and industry possible.

01

Farmer Input Financing

Structured "Supply-Now, Pay-After-Harvest" credit models where smallholder farmers receive critical certified fertilizer inputs during planting season and settle accounts post-harvest — shielding rural families from hyperinflation and cash flow distress.

02

Free Agronomic Education

Practical field demonstration days across rural LGAs in Northern Nigeria teach optimal micro-dosing fertilizer placement, soil nutrient matching, and timing — reducing input wastage, increasing harvest yield, and curbing runoff.

03

Youth Employment & Apprenticeships

Scholarships, apprenticeships, and vocational training at Rusbi Skills Academy for underserved youths in Kano State, creating skilled tradesmen, certified solar/machinery technicians, and local entrepreneurs.
